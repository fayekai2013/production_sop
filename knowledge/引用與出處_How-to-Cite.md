## Rules for reuse / 沿用規則

- Cite the TECH ID and this package when you rely on a dossier; do not cite the private library as if it were publicly retrievable.
- The evidence grades in the workflow's Part 5 apply to practices, not to these dossiers; a dossier is research material, and its own text states its sources and limits.
- If you redistribute, keep this file with the dossiers. A dossier without its provenance is a claim without a source.

引用時標 TECH 編號與本包，不要把私人庫寫成公開可查的來源；再散佈時，這份出處說明要跟 dossier 一起走。
