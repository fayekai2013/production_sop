# Specialist Analysis and Independent QC

## Dispatch by domain

- Split every analysis job by domain before work starts, such as data, media, content and copy, social, audience psychology and industry, and assign each part to a specialist working only in that domain.

- Keep collectors separate from analysts. Collectors gather material and record its source, and analysts interpret only material that carries a source.

- Attach a source to every number and factual claim, with the source name, the link, the access date and the original wording it rests on.

- Write not found when a fact cannot be found, and label anything inferred as an inference instead of presenting it as a finding.

- Integrate the specialist findings without rewriting their conclusions. Place conflicting findings side by side and name the conflict instead of smoothing it away.

## Fix the comparison design before coding

- Decide the single corpus for each coded comparison before any coding starts, and code every compared item from that same corpus.

- Compare like with like. Compare advertisements with advertisements and websites with websites, and never place the two in one comparison or on one page as if they were the same surface.

- Label each chart and page with the corpus and surface it uses, so a reader can see whether it shows paid first contact material or an owned surface.

- Build a positioning quadrant from the products themselves. Decompose each product into attributes such as price, stated claims, form and portability, charging, measurement and feedback, and certification or refund terms, code those attributes, and calculate the coordinates from the coding.

- Call coding of advertisement copy a diagnostic under its own name. Do not deliver it as a positioning quadrant.

- Label the denominator on every share and score. Exclude an item with a zero denominator from that chart, note the exclusion and its reason on the chart, and never force a coordinate for it.

## Keep coverage even across compared items

- List every compared brand or item and the sections it must receive before building the deliverable, covering its data analysis, its charts and its representative material.

- Check coverage against that list before delivery. A missing section or missing material wall for one compared item counts as unfinished work, not as a shorter report.

- Give every pairing the same treatment in split comparisons, with the base item present in each chart and the same axes and scale across all of them.

## Run independent QC on the deliverable itself

- Send the finished deliverable to an independent QC reviewer who did not make it. The maker never acts as the final QC reviewer.

- Have QC check integrity and credibility, specialist overreach beyond the evidence, and any place where integration changed a specialist conclusion.

- Have QC open every image in the deliverable at full size and check that its brand, subject and surface match the page, the caption and the argument it supports. A thumbnail check does not count.

- Have QC recheck corpus consistency, denominators, coverage parity and chart calculations against the underlying data before it passes the deliverable.

- Claim completion only after QC passes, or after every blocker from a conditional pass is closed and rechecked. Report any remaining limitation plainly with the delivery.

## Alternatives for better results

- Narrow the comparison set to items with a complete and comparable corpus rather than widening it with items that can only be coded from summaries.

- Deliver the underlying coded data beside the presentation when a conclusion depends on coding choices, so the user can audit the coordinates and shares.

- Stop and report the gap when one compared item has no current material in the chosen corpus, and treat that absence itself as a market fact worth reporting.
