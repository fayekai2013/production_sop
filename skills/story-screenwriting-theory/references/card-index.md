# Card index — directory (routing text added 2026-11-10; the section introduction below is the original §4 text, verbatim)

## 4. Card index

The complete index of the knowledge base, routed by design problem. Routes R1–R15 are the same routes used by the diagnosis workflow in §3. Every card in the knowledge base appears exactly once, under its package's primary route. Each entry gives the card ID, the card title as written on the card face, the evidence grade, the flags carried on the entry itself (EVIDENCE_THIN, SINGLE_SOURCE, and any combined-basis or transmitted-basis notes — a flag in this index is part of the citation, per §7.5), and the path of the card body. Artefact indexes (mechanism map, glossary, Murch register, action verbs, case breakdowns) close the routes they belong to. One package, structural_comparison, is a comparative-critique document rather than a card package and is indexed as an artefact under R1.
